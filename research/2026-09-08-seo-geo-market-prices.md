# US market prices for SEO and GEO work, and the floors set from them, 2026

Date: 2026-09-08. Method: WebSearch + WebFetch sweep of 2026 pricing guides, the Ahrefs provider survey, marketplace listings and agency rate pages, done the day the floors changed.
Evidence tiers as in [2026-08-19-ai-deliverable-fixed-price-bands.md](2026-08-19-ai-deliverable-fixed-price-bands.md): [PA] platform or large-n data, [NM] news/industry media, [OWNER] vendor or agency blog (interest-laden, anchors high).

Ask that started it: price the SEO and GEO services at the market rate, slightly under it. Before this note the SEO page carried one figure, from $300, with AI search folded in and no price of its own.

Reading rule, carried over: the site publishes floors ("from $X"), so the honest comparison is floor to floor. The $50-125 gig tier and the $99/mo "AI visibility" subscriptions are the commodity floor and are not the market a US business owner compares a named developer against. The floor a buyer would recognise as professional is the lowest figure the guides give for a freelancer or an entry-level program.

## 1. SEO: one-time technical audit or pass on an existing site
- [OWNER] rteetech (2026-01-27): "Freelancer or Lite SEO Audits" $500-2,500 (technical overview, limited content review); technical audits $500-5,000 by site size; agency full audits $2,500-7,500; enterprise $10,000-30,000+. https://blog.rteetech.com/seo-audit-pricing/
- [OWNER] Lengreo (2026-04-13): audits $500-5,000; basic technical audit for sites under 100 pages $500-1,500; standard comprehensive $1,000-3,000; most SMBs pay $750-2,500; on-page audit $300-2,500; hourly $50-300 with most experienced specialists at $100-200; India/emerging-market agencies from $300-800. https://lengreo.com/seo-audit-pricing/
- [OWNER] AgencyAnalytics (2025-08-14): basic audits on sites under 20 pages $500-1,000; Fiverr audits under $100; hourly $50-150+, up to $250 for audits. https://agencyanalytics.com/blog/how-much-to-charge-seo-audit
- [OWNER] The Business Rover (updated 2026-08-28): local SEO audit $1,500-5,000; GBP setup $500-2,000; citation audit/cleanup $300-1,500; local landing page $300-800 per page. https://www.thebusinessrover.com/local-seo-pricing
- [PA] Marketplace gig tier: Fiverr "full technical SEO audit" gigs at $125, "complete audit" gigs at $50 (search results 2026-09-08); Upwork SEO expert median $21/hr, typical $15-35/hr (Upwork cost page, via search snippet; the page itself returns 403 to fetches).
- Floor a US buyer recognises: **$500** (four independent guides put the freelancer/basic technical tier at $500).

## 2. SEO: ongoing monthly work
- [PA] Ahrefs provider survey (439 providers, 2023, updated 2024-08-15): most common retainer $501-1,000/mo (20.4%); 42.8% charge $501-2,000/mo; most common hourly $75-100; freelancers average $1,348.63/mo against agencies at $3,209/mo; most common per-project fee $2,501-5,000. https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/
- [OWNER] The Business Rover (2026-08-28): GBP management only $500-1,500/mo; single-location local SEO $750-4,000/mo. https://www.thebusinessrover.com/local-seo-pricing
- [OWNER] Lengreo (2026-04-13): ongoing SEO in the US/UK/Canada/Western Europe averages $1,000-2,500/mo.
- [NM/OWNER] Search sweep 2026-09-08 (Coalition, Digital Elevator, WebFX local pricing, seoprofy, arc4): freelancers typically $500-1,500/mo for a single-location business; average across all providers ~$2,917/mo; SE Ranking reports 56.2% of agencies raised rates into 2026 citing inflation and AI search.
- Floor a US buyer recognises: **$500/mo**.

## 3. GEO / AI search: one-time audit or pass
- [OWNER] Fisher Design & Advertising (2026-06-01): one-time AI visibility audits $1,500-5,000; standard $1,500-2,500 (20-30 queries across 3-4 AI platforms); comprehensive $3,000-5,000 (50+ queries, 5-10 competitors); AI-optimised content $400-1,200 per piece. https://www.fisherdesignandadvertising.com/how-much-does-ai-search-optimization-cost/
- [OWNER] Digital Elevator (updated 2026-07-23): freelance/gig work $150-2,000 per project; their own AI Visibility audit from $3,000. https://thedigitalelevator.com/blog/aeo-and-geo-pricing-guide/
- [OWNER] Lengreo (2026-04-13): agencies that add generative engine optimisation to an audit charge $500-1,000 more than SEO-only, which puts an SMB audit with GEO at about $1,500 and up.
- [OWNER] WebFX (2026-05-26) and Nutshell (2026-04-22): project-based GEO $5,000-50,000; hourly $50-300. https://www.webfx.com/blog/ai/generative-engine-optimization-cost/ ; https://www.nutshell.com/blog/geo-costs
- Floor a US buyer recognises: **$1,500** for an audit alone. The market figure buys a report; the pass sold here also implements the fixes.

- [NM/OWNER] Search sweep (TeamAI, Stackmatix, Cited Co, Soar): entry-level programs $1,000-2,500/mo; hybrid tool licence plus part-time specialist $1,500-3,000/mo; self-serve multi-engine tracking $200-500/mo.
