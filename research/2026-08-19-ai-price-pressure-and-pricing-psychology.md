# AI price pressure on small-business software work + pricing psychology for slightly-below-market positioning

Date: 2026-08-19. Method: WebSearch + WebFetch sweep, 2025-2026 sources preferred.
Evidence tiers: [PR] peer-reviewed/academic, [PA] platform/financial data or large-n survey, [NM] news/industry media, [OWNER] vendor/agency blog (interest-laden).

## PART A — Did prices for small-business software work fall?

### Evidence prices FELL
- [PR] Hui, Reshef, Zhou (Upwork data; publicized through 2025): AI-exposed freelancers saw ~2% fewer contracts and ~5% lower monthly earnings post-ChatGPT; earnings fell more than jobs (work devalued, not just displaced). Top performers NOT insulated — disproportionately affected. https://www.brookings.edu/articles/is-generative-ai-a-job-killer-evidence-from-the-freelance-market/ ; https://phys.org/news/2025-03-generative-ai-upending-freelance-safe.html ; https://www.informs.org/News-Room/INFORMS-Releases/News-Releases/Generative-AI-Is-Upending-Freelance-Work-Even-Top-Performers-Aren-t-Safe
- [PR] Harvard/Imperial (2M postings, 61 countries): automation-prone freelance postings -21% post-ChatGPT; writing -30.4%, software/app dev -20.6%, graphic design -17%. Via https://www.winvesta.in/blog/freelancers/ai-cut-freelance-rates-30-how-top-earners-fight-back
- [PA] Vollna/GigRadar Upwork Market Report (2.2M projects): writing projects -32% YoY 2025; 11 of 12 categories declined; entry-level project share fell from 15% to <9%. https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-market-report-2026
- [PA] Fiverr Q2 2026: revenue -10% YoY ($97.8M vs $108.6M); marketplace revenue -15.5%; FY guide implies -14–17%; "Generative AI is absorbing the quick, commoditized tasks." Active buyers -13.6% YoY in Q4 2025; ~30% workforce laid off Sep 2025. https://www.selfemployed.com/news/fiverr-q2-2026-earnings-ai-freelance/ ; https://insights.itdukes.com/insights/upwork-fiverr-decline
- [NM] DevTechInsights: avg freelance dev hourly $75 (2022) → $48 (mid-2025), -36%; frontend down "up to 40%"; anecdote: $300 SEO-script projects now get $120 offers (client says "ChatGPT could do this"). No primary citations — treat as directional. https://devtechinsights.com/why-developers-are-earning-less-2025-ai-impact/
- [OWNER] Arc/index.dev-adjacent claim: routine maintenance work rates softened 5-10%; project totals fell because AI speeds delivery even where hourly held. https://www.techradar.com/pro/chatgpt-has-caused-a-massive-drop-in-demand-for-online-digital-freelancers-here-is-what-you-can-do-to-protect-yourself
- [OWNER] AI website builders as substitute floor: $12-50/mo vs $3k-7k custom builds ("save 80-90%"); Bolt "save $20K". https://durable.com/blog/how-much-does-an-ai-website-builder-cost ; https://bolt.new/blog/save-20k-build-your-business-website-with-ai
- [OWNER] Agency side: "AI is collapsing the cost of writing code… commoditized in real time"; deliverable timelines compressed 3-4x, fewer billable hours. https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-pricing-models-2026-decision-guide

### Evidence prices HELD or ROSE
- [PA] Productive.io pulse (180+ agencies, 2025→2026): 73% never asked for an AI discount; only 13% actually lowered rates; feared discount wave "still hasn't arrived." https://productive.io/reports/agencies-in-the-ai-era-pulse-report/ ; https://techbullion.com/the-discounting-myth-why-most-agencies-arent-losing-money-to-ai-competition/
- [PA] Upwork 2025: AI-project freelancers earn +44%/hr vs non-AI; AI rates +60% YoY; AI work >$300M annualized; 52% of GSV growth. https://www.upwork.com/resources/freelancing-stats
- [PA] Memvers (1,247 transactions, Fiverr/Upwork/Toptal, Q4'25-Q1'26): AI services +68% avg price since 2024 — chatbot setup $350→$600 (+71%), workflow automation $250→$400 (+60%), custom GPT/Claude agent $150→$300 (+100%), RAG setup $500→$800 (+60%). Current medians: landing page $350, WP 5-10p $900, Shopify $1,200, React app $4,500, logo $150. https://memvers.com/blog/freelance-pricing-data-study-2026
- [PA] YunoJuno 2025 (UK): avg day rate +3% YoY to £390; top 10% £708/day (+9%). https://www.yunojuno.com/blogs/ai-skills-drive-shift-in-contingent-workforce-market-as-average-day-rates-rise
- [PA] Web Designer Academy survey (n≈200, Mar-Apr 2025): most designers still charge $2,500-$9,999/project, cluster $2,500-5,000, ~$100/hr; no reported YoY fall. https://webdesigneracademy.com/the-state-of-web-designer-pricing-in-2025-freelance-rates-survey-insights-industry-trends/
- [NM] Mid-market redesign spend: median $19,850 for midsize-business redesign, +13% YoY (2025 analysis). https://silverspoonagency.com/average-website-design-cost/
- [NM] Barbell effect: $25 blog posts → $250 ghostwritten essays; $50 logos → $400 brand identities; $200-600 niche logo designers "booked solid" while generic collapsed. https://memvers.com/blog/freelance-pricing-data-study-2026 ; https://unil.ink/blog/best-fiverr-gigs-2026

### Verdict (recorded)
Yes at the commodity end, no at the trusted-practitioner end. The floor (basic sites, simple bots, small scripts, writing) fell 30-60% or vanished into DIY tools; the platform middle eroded (all marketplace metrics down); custom/relationship work held or rose, and AI-implementation work is the fastest-riser (+60-100%). Position accordingly: sell the risen categories (automation, chatbots, AI integration) at healthy prices; treat "basic website" as a loss-leader door, not a profit line.

## PART B — Pricing psychology for slightly-below-market

1. Undercut shallow (~10-15%), never deep. Deep discounts trigger price-quality inference (Rao & Monroe meta-analysis, 36 studies). Journal of Retailing 2021: discount depth vs quality-uncertainty is inverted-U; small discounts are safest. Worked example in the wild: $22/hr vs $25 market ≈ 12% under. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022435921000786 ; https://acuityscheduling.com/learn/how-to-price-your-services
2. Publish "from $X" starting prices: builds trust, pre-qualifies, filters price-shoppers; anchor with the expensive option shown first (CXL eye-tracking: first/leftmost expensive raises choice of pricier tiers; decoy +43% Ariely). https://kejoola.com/blog/should-you-put-prices-on-website-service-business ; https://www.invespcro.com/blog/price-anchoring/
3. Endings: round numbers signal quality/emotional confidence; 9-endings signal deal and (implicit-measure study, Springer 2026) lower quality. B2B quotes: precise non-round anchors ($1,850 style) read as informed and draw smaller counteroffers (Janiszewski & Uy; Mason et al.), but back off precision with expert buyers (too-much-precision effect). https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/s41272-026-00578-5 ; https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/dealmaking-daily/negotiation-research-can-use-effective-first-offer-strive-precision-nb
4. Entry tier: $27-97 tripwire-range for business audiences; one-hour fixed-scope "small fix" fits; keep tier gaps 1.4-1.8x (mid) and 2-3x (top) so the entry can't substitute for a project; ~50-66% pick middle of three. https://7milemedia.com/post/tripwire-funnel-ideas-service-businesses/ ; https://ahmmedauthororiginalpricing.medium.com/good-better-best-pricing-the-complete-guide-to-tiered-portfolio-strategies-2be8e07585e6 ; https://www.getjobber.com/academy/good-better-best-pricing-examples/
5. Paid discovery doubles $5k+ close rate (only 15% of designers do it) — an entry tier that IS discovery converts and upsells. https://webdesigneracademy.com/the-state-of-web-designer-pricing-in-2025-freelance-rates-survey-insights-industry-trends/

### What this does NOT establish
- No controlled study pins an exact "X% below market = low-quality signal" threshold; the 10-15% rule is triangulated from inverted-U discount research + practitioner examples, not measured directly for dev services.
- DevTechInsights' $75→$48 lacks primary sourcing; do not quote as fact, only as a widely-circulated claim.
- Platform declines conflate AI substitution with macro softness and platform-specific churn.
