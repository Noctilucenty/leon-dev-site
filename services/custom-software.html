questions people ask first

Questions before starting

how do i know custom is worth it vs off-the-shelf?

if an existing product does 90% of what you need, buy it — we'll tell you so in the free call. custom wins when the missing part is what your business actually runs on.

what does custom software cost?

full builds start at $1,500 and scale with scope. quotes are written and fixed, with staged payments tied to milestones you can see working.

can another developer take over later?

yes. the agreed repo and project accounts, included source code and setup documentation are handed over. third-party hosts and licensed services keep their terms, and another competent developer can use the handoff — nothing is set up to make us irreplaceable.
