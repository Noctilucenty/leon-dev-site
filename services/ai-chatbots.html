Scope, examples & what is included

customers often ask the same questions: hours, prices, availability, 'do you do X'. a chatbot can help answer them after hours and in multiple languages, using the information you approve.

we ground the assistant in your approved information, test its answers, and give it a clear route to a person when it is unsure. ai can still make mistakes, so sensitive decisions stay with your staff. the assistant on this site is one Leon built.

What we build for this

  • a chatbot grounded in your approved business information
  • written handoff rules and a way for visitors to reach a person
  • lead capture so visitors can request a reply

Questions before starting

what does a chatbot cost?

from $750 for a site chatbot trained on your business. connecting it to booking or your other systems adds scope — fixed quote before anything starts.

will it say something wrong to a customer?

it can. we reduce that risk with approved source material, answer testing, clear limits, and human handoff. prices, policies, and sensitive decisions need explicit rules; an ai assistant should never be treated as infallible.

can it capture leads?

yes — we can add a short handoff that collects the visitor's project details and contact information for your reply. the assistant on this site shows that flow.
