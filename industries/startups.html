questions people ask first

Questions from startups owners

how fast can an mvp ship?

weeks, not quarters — scope decides. we cut to the version that tests the idea, in writing, before building.

do i own the code?

the agreed repo and project accounts, plus the source code and setup notes included in scope, are handed over. third-party infrastructure, app stores, libraries and other licensed services keep their own terms. investors can review the written handoff.

can you keep building after launch?

yes — ongoing from $400/mo, or fixed quotes per milestone as you raise.
