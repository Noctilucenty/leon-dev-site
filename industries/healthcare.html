questions people ask first

Questions from medical & dental owners

is this hipaa compliant?

builds are designed so sensitive data stays in your existing systems wherever possible, and we're direct about what we will and won't touch. compliance requirements get scoped in writing before anything starts — never assumed.

will ai talk to my patients about medical questions?

the assistant is scoped for routine logistics, not medical advice. we agree on limits and staff handoff, then test them before launch. those controls reduce risk but do not make model answers infallible.
