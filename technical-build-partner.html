Before we start.

Do I need a technical brief?

No. Describe the problem in your own words. Requesting a quote is free, and a call or paid plan is optional.

How quickly can I see progress?

For a ready, well-scoped project, a first reviewable milestone can be targeted within 1–3 business days. This is not the full build. The full schedule is agreed after checking scope and access.

What is included in my quote?

The agreed work, testing, source code, account access, setup notes, and support period. Hosting, paid tools, advertising, data cleanup, ongoing content, and extra work are separate. Third-party licenses keep their own terms.

How does monthly work stay on track?

We agree on capacity, exclusions, and how unfinished work carries over before the month starts. You get a weekly progress note and a monthly review. This is not unlimited development.
