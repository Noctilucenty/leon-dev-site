the questions that come first

i already have a website. do i have to start over?

usually not. most sites have one real problem — slow, broken on a phone, nobody can update it, or it doesn't take bookings. i'll tell you which parts are worth keeping. a rebuild only makes sense when fixing costs more than replacing, and i'll show you that comparison instead of asserting it.

who owns the code and the accounts?

you do. repo, domain, hosting, api keys and the store listing all go in your name. if we stop working together nothing switches off and nothing has to be rebuilt to get away from me.

how long does it take?

a business site is usually one to two weeks. an integration or automation, a few days to two weeks. a booking system or portal, three to six weeks. a full mobile app, longer, and that depends on what it does rather than how fast anyone types. you get a real estimate with the quote, not a number invented on this page.

do you disappear after launch?

fixes are included for 30 days on a fixed project and 90 on a full build. after that you can keep me on a monthly block or just message me when something comes up. plenty of people do the second one.

can you work with the tools we already use?

yes, and it's usually cheaper. square, toast, quickbooks, stripe, shopify, google workspace, notion, slack, twilio, your pos or your ats. connecting what you already pay for normally beats replacing it.

is ai actually worth it for a business my size?

sometimes. it's genuinely good at reading, drafting, classifying and answering from your own documents. it's a bad idea anywhere a confident wrong answer costs you a customer, which is why the systems i build put a person in front of anything public, and why i'll talk you out of the ai version when a boring script does the job better.

i don't know what i need. is that a problem?

no. it's the most common starting point. describe the part of the week that annoys you most and we'll work backwards from there.

why is everything on this site lowercase?

because i like it. the estimates, the invoices and the code are punctuated normally.
