Before starting

Four practical questions.

How much will my project cost?

A limited business presence site starts at $300, website plus follow-up starts at $1,500, and one defined automation starts at $500. The written quote depends on scope and is fixed before work begins.

How long will it take?

The contractor website and follow-up product targets 10 business days once scope, access, and approved copy are ready. Other timelines depend on the work and are written into the proposal.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Usually. I first check whether your current tools can be configured or connected before recommending a replacement.

What happens after launch?

You receive the agreed handoff and support terms. Third-party services, licensed components, and provider fees keep their own terms.

Start with the business problem

Tell me what is slowing the business down.

Plain words are enough. I will tell you whether software is the right fix and what a sensible first version looks like.
