The questions that come first.

I do not know what software I need. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Start with the part of the week that is manual, broken, or frustrating. I will work backward from the outcome and recommend the smallest sensible approach.

Who owns the code and project accounts?

The written scope names the repository, deliverables, data, and project accounts included in handover. They are set up in your name where the provider allows it; third-party services and licensed components keep their own terms.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A focused website or integration may take days or a few weeks; a portal or full app takes longer. Your proposal includes a real timeline before work begins.

What support is included after launch?

The proposal states the fix period, response path, and any ongoing support. You receive the agreed handover materials so another qualified developer can also maintain the project.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Usually. I first check whether your current tools can be connected or configured before recommending a replacement. Provider access, compatibility, licenses, and fees are confirmed during scope.
