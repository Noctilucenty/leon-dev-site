# Non-Bay contractor Search metro research — 2026-08-24

**Status: RESEARCH ONLY — NO LAUNCH AUTHORIZATION — DO NOT ALTER THE BAY AREA CAMPAIGN**

This note preserves a comparable Keyword Planner pass and official Census
evidence for possible future, separately authorized contractor-website and
lead-recovery tests. It does not authorize creating a campaign, changing a
location, moving budget, publishing an ad, or replacing the existing nine-county
Bay Area control.

## Research conclusion

If a separate non-Bay test is authorized later, the evidence supports this
order:

1. **Orlando first.** It produced the strongest standardized Planner forecast:
   4.54 clicks from 70.15 impressions at a forecast $3.89 average CPC. The metro
   also has 8,635 construction establishments and ranked sixth among metropolitan
   CBSAs for 2025 privately-owned housing units authorized.
2. **Phoenix second.** It forecast 2.61 clicks at a lower $3.16 average CPC and
   has the largest verified construction base in the shortlist: 10,303
   establishments. Its first two visible contractor-design terms were marked
   High competition, so it follows Orlando rather than displacing it.
3. **Charlotte as an underserved-market hypothesis, not a forecast winner.**
   It has 7,500 construction establishments and 22,233 housing units authorized,
   while all three visible terms were marked Medium competition. However, its
   forecast was only 0.55 clicks from 13.39 impressions. “Underserved” is an
   inference to test, not a Planner or Census finding.

Dallas and Houston remain demand controls in this pass. Their standardized
forecasts do not support moving either ahead of Orlando or Phoenix, and a full
comparable Census profile was not captured for them.

- Construction establishments are direct 2023 County Business Patterns MSA

  privately-owned housing units authorized**, not counts of contractors,

## Limits and decision boundary

- The forecast window is **September 1–30**, not the contemplated Bay Area
  flight of **August 25–September 3**. A 30-day Planner forecast is not a
  prediction of delivery during that 10-day flight and should not be prorated
  into a promised result.
- No Orlando, Phoenix, Charlotte, Dallas, or Houston campaign was created. No
  alternate-metro location, keyword, ad, budget, bid, schedule, or billing
  setting was applied.
- No change was made or is recommended here to the existing Bay Area campaign.
  Any future metro test needs its own authorization, landing-page and ad review,
  current Planner validation, budget decision, conversion checks, and stop
  rules.
- The forecast counts are very small. They rank hypotheses under one captured
  setup; they do not establish statistical demand or probable client volume.
- Construction-establishment, population, and permit counts describe metro
  scale and activity. They do not prove that contractors need a website, will
  search these phrases, or will hire Leon Builds.
- Competition labels and historical bid ranges describe the Google Ads market,
  not the number or quality of web developers serving a metro. Charlotte's
  underserved-market label remains a hypothesis until a separately authorized
  test produces qualified-call and won-client evidence.
