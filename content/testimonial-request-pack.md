# Testimonial request and approval pack

**Status: TEMPLATE — CUSTOMER APPROVAL REQUIRED**

This file creates a repeatable way to collect genuine client feedback. It is not
permission to invent, improve, or publish a quote. Keep completed approvals,
screenshots, email threads, and any claim evidence in `private/testimonials/`,
which is gitignored. Never commit a client's private contact details.

## Short request

**Subject:** Could I quote your feedback about [PROJECT]?

> Hi [NAME] — I am updating the project examples on my website. Would you be
> comfortable sharing two or three honest sentences about what I built for
> [BUSINESS/PROJECT], what the process was like, and what changed afterward?
> Positive, mixed, or critical feedback is all useful. I will send the exact
> wording and attribution back to you before anything is published.

## Three useful prompts

1. What was difficult, manual, or missing before the project?
2. What did Leon build, and what was it like working directly with him?
3. What changed after launch? Include a number only if you can verify it and are
   comfortable having it published.

## Approval-ready quote frames

These are fill-in structures, not testimonials. Replace every bracket with the
client's own facts and voice, then send the exact result back for approval.

### Problem to result

> Before this project, [SPECIFIC PROBLEM]. Leon built [SPECIFIC DELIVERABLE].
> Now [TRUTHFUL OUTCOME THAT THE CLIENT CAN SUPPORT].

### Direct working relationship

> Leon understood [BUSINESS NEED] and made [PART OF THE PROCESS] straightforward.
> I especially appreciated [SPECIFIC BEHAVIOR OR DELIVERABLE].

### Handover and ownership

> We received [AGREED CODE/ACCOUNTS/SETUP NOTES], and [TRUTHFUL HANDOVER OUTCOME].
> The scope and additions were [CLIENT'S HONEST DESCRIPTION].

## Exact publication approval

Send this after drafting the final excerpt:

> Here is the exact text I would like to publish:
>
> “[EXACT QUOTE]”
>
> Attribution: [NAME] — [ROLE/BUSINESS OR APPROVED PROJECT LABEL]
>
> Rating shown, if any: [NONE OR EXACT CLIENT-PROVIDED RATING]
>
> Placement: leonbuilds.org and related project marketing
>
> Please reply **Approved** if the quote, attribution, rating, and placement are
> accurate and you authorize publication. Please send any edits instead if they
> are not. Also tell me about any incentive or relationship that should be
> disclosed.

## Private evidence checklist

- Original feedback in the client's own words.
- The exact final excerpt sent back to the client.
- Client approval date and approved attribution level.
- Exact rating source, if a rating will be shown.
- Evidence for measurable results or counts.
- Any material relationship or incentive disclosure.
- A contact path for corrections or withdrawal requests.

Do not condition an incentive on positive sentiment, suppress critical feedback,
or change the meaning while shortening a quote. If a measurable claim cannot be
supported, remove the number before requesting final approval.
