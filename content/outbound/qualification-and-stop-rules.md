# Qualification rubric and stop rules

**Status: DRAFT — UNSENT**

**NO SEND AUTHORIZATION.** This rubric governs a proposed workflow and contains
no lead or customer record.

## Fifteen-minute call structure

1. What happens today from the first customer action to the completed handoff?
2. Where does it wait, get copied, get lost, or depend on one person remembering?
3. How often does that happen, and what does the owner already measure?
4. Which tool, account, or staff rule cannot be replaced?
5. Who decides, who uses the result, and when would a change matter?
6. Restate the smallest plausible next step, including “keep the current tool”
   when that is the honest answer.

Do not diagnose regulated data handling, security, accessibility, payments, or
messaging compliance beyond the evidence available. Flag it for separate review.

## Score, zero to two per dimension

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem evidence | Vague wish or software-first request. | One plausible pain but no concrete example. | Current workflow and failure point are specific. |
| Frequency or stakes | Rare and low consequence. | Recurring or meaningful, not yet measured. | Frequent or material with owner-provided evidence. |
| Decision access | No route to owner or authorized lead. | Influencer can arrange access. | Decision maker is participating. |
| Timing | No reason to act. | A future window exists. | A real current deadline or operating need exists. |
| Delivery fit | Unknown system or uncontrolled dependency. | Feasible after one validation step. | Clear fit with controllable scope. |
| Commercial fit | Expects free work or cannot discuss scope. | Budget path is unclear but credible. | Understands written scope and canonical starting points. |
| Collaboration fit | Withholds basics or demands guarantees. | Normal uncertainty. | Shares constraints and accepts staged decisions. |

Maximum: 14.

- 10–14 and no mandatory stop: qualified; prepare the smallest scoped next step.
- 7–9: diagnostic/nurture only; resolve the missing evidence before a proposal.
- 0–6: no proposal; recommend a resource or close politely.

Mandatory proposal gates: authorized decision path, lawful and ethical request,
credible delivery fit, explicit problem, and acceptance that scope and price are
agreed in writing before work.

## Individual-contact stop rules

Stop immediately and suppress future outreach when:

- The person opts out, asks not to be contacted, complains, or says the contact
  channel is personal/inappropriate.
- The address is invalid, belongs to an unrelated person, or was obtained through
  scraping, guessing, purchase, or a source whose permitted use is unclear.
- A partner or referrer did not have permission to share private information.
- The observation is no longer true or cannot be verified.
- Continuing would require impersonation, a fake inquiry, bypassing access, or
  an unsupported claim.

Stop the cold sequence after Touch 4 without a reply. “Not now” receives a future
follow-up only when the recipient explicitly requests a specific time.

## Channel stop rules

Pause a channel before new activity when:

- A platform warning, restriction, removal pattern, or moderator instruction
  suggests the format or placement is not allowed.
- Two community posts are declined for the same placement/type. Re-read the rules;
  do not keep rewriting the same promotion.
- Any complaint exposes a systemic targeting, data-source, sender-identity, or
  opt-out problem.
- Source attribution is missing, duplicated, or encodes personal data.
- The current valid postal address, suppression process, or compliance review is
  not ready for commercial email.

## Experiment stop and change rules

These are internal decision gates, not outside-industry benchmarks:

- After 75 comparable accounts receive two careful touches, fewer than three
  qualified positive replies means reframe the problem, proof, or niche before
  adding volume.
- Positive replies but almost no bookings means simplify the handoff: answer the
  question, then offer two times and the calendar link as an alternative.
- Bookings but repeated no-shows means shorten time-to-meeting and audit reminder
  clarity before increasing traffic.
- Held calls below the qualification threshold mean targeting is too broad.
- Qualified calls without proposals mean the offer or call diagnosis is unclear.
- Five relevant proposals without a win triggers a review of proof, scope, price,
  risk, and decision access; it does not trigger a discount by default.
- Views, impressions, likes, and opens never override weak qualified-call data.

## Capacity gate

Do not scale organic volume or paid traffic until:

- At least five qualified calls have been held using the same core offer.
- At least two appropriate proposals have been produced.
- Reply, booking, proposal, and delivery follow-up can be handled within one
  business day without harming current client work.
- The source can be tracked without personal data in the URL.
- There is a clear stop condition for spend or volume.

Calendar utilization is not the objective. Qualified held calls, appropriate
proposals, wins, delivery capacity, and revenue are the decision metrics.
