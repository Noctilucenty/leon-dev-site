# Thirty-day organic and outbound calendar

**Status: DRAFT — UNSENT**

**NO SEND AUTHORIZATION.** Tasks below describe a proposed operating rhythm.
They do not assert that outreach, posting, meetings, or account work occurred.

## Daily operating block

- Research: up to 60 minutes; reject records without a specific observable fact.
- Draft and review: up to 45 minutes; one-to-one, no mail merge.
- Follow-up and replies: up to 45 minutes; opt-outs first.
- Warm/partner/community work: 30 minutes.
- CRM and evidence: 15 minutes; record outcomes, not vanity metrics.
- Calls and proposals: separate protected blocks; reply with two specific times
  rather than forcing every interested person through a calendar link.

Do not cold-email on weekends merely to hit a quota. Use non-business days for
research, draft review, case-study preparation, and measurement unless a recipient
has already asked for a reply.

## Month-one activity envelope

- 75 equal bakeoff accounts.
- Up to 120 additional accounts in the winning wedge, only if stop rules permit.
- 20–30 specific warm-introduction requests over the month.
- Up to 20 partner requests, paced at roughly five per week.
- Six useful proof/process community drafts, each checked against current rules.
- Two relevant local events or organizer conversations.
- Up to ten bespoke audits reserved for the highest-fit records.

These are capacity limits, not send quotas. Fewer well-researched records are
better than filling the CRM with weak fits.

## Weekly scoreboard

Track raw counts and rates for: accepted research records, valid contact paths,
touches reviewed, touches actually authorized and used, replies, qualified
positive replies, booked calls, held calls, qualified calls, proposals, wins,
revenue, opt-outs, complaints, and platform warnings.

The operating goal is a repeatable stream of qualified conversations. A full
calendar containing poor-fit or no-show calls is not success.
