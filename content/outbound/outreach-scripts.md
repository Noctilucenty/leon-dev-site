# Warm, referral, and direct-outreach scripts

**Status: DRAFT — UNSENT**

**NO SEND AUTHORIZATION.** Every bracketed field requires manual research and
review. Cold-email drafts intentionally retain `[VALID POSTAL ADDRESS]`, so they
are not send-ready. This is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## Usage rules

- Never invent familiarity, a referral, a prior reply, urgency, or a measured
  business loss.
- Use `Re:` only when the message is actually in the same email thread.
- An observation describes a current public page. An interpretation uses words
  such as “may” or “might.”
- Do not submit a fake inquiry, call while pretending to be a customer, or access
  a private system to manufacture personalization.
- Use a recipient's language only when it has been identified appropriately and
  the final message has been manually reviewed. Do not bulk-translate a list.
- No cold SMS, WhatsApp, WeChat, automated DM, bought list, or scraped-address run.
- Append the compliance footer to every commercial email. Check current federal,
  state, platform, and mailbox-provider requirements separately before use.
- Stop immediately on opt-out. Stop after Touch 4 when there is no reply.

are not for Leon to impersonate a referrer.

## Reply handling

- Interested: answer the question first, then offer two specific times plus the
  booking link as an alternative.
- “Send information”: send only the promised relevant sketch or proof, with its
  source tag; do not attach a generic deck.
- Existing tool works: acknowledge it and close unless they ask for integration.
- Not now: ask whether they want the thread closed or a date-specific follow-up;
  silence is not permission for an indefinite nurture sequence.
- No, remove, unsubscribe, complaint, or hostile response: mark do-not-contact
  immediately, acknowledge once if appropriate, and stop.
