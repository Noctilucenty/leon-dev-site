LOQOL seller portal case study.

Real-estate disclosures take time to complete. Leon built parts of LOQOL's existing seller portal that connect answers to the right records and protect the text a seller is still writing.

Role: Frontend developer contributing to an existing product and design · Client product contribution · reviewed September 6, 2026

The decision this solved

Let the seller finish typing before replacing their answer.

A seller is halfway through an answer when the page fetches an older saved copy. If the form replaces the text too soon, their unfinished work can disappear. Leon's work focused on that moment: keeping the answer being typed separate from the last saved version, and connecting each field to the right record.

What Leon built

What Leon worked on.

01

Keep answers with the right record

Connected disclosure forms to the system that loads and updates each seller's answers.

02

Request saves as sellers work

Added automatic save requests when typing pauses, the seller moves to another field, or they close an unfinished form.

03

Protect the answer in progress

Kept the seller's current text separate from older saved answers arriving in the background.
